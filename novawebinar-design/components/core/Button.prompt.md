Fully-rounded pill button; use accent (orange #E8440C, white text) for the primary action, light/white for everything else.
```jsx
<Button variant="accent" iconRight="arrow-up-right">Export as CVS</Button>
<Button variant="light" icon="plus">Add widget</Button>
```
- Variants: accent (primary), light (on canvas), white (on light cards), dark, ghost. Sizes sm 32 / md 40 / lg 48.
- Hover darkens one step; press scales to .97.
